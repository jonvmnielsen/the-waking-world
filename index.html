How to play

Hero: tap the Hero button (top left) for your hero's overview, or tap the hero. With the hero selected, tap the ground to walk, an enemy to attack, an item or chest to pick it up. Health, mana and experience show above your hero.

Deselect: tap ✕ Deselect (bottom left) so nothing is selected — then you can look around without moving anyone.

Workers: tap a worker, then tap a gold mine, any tree or any stone to gather. Use the panel to build. "Idle" selects a worker with nothing to do.

Army: tap a soldier to select it, double-tap to select all of that kind, or hold your finger still for a moment and drag a box. "Army" selects every soldier. With a War Camp selected, tapping the ground sets where new soldiers gather.

Ascension: at level 10 your hero can Ascend and choose one of three paths, with new abilities up to level 20. The path that matches how you fight is free. Enemies far below your level give less experience; stronger enemies give more.

Upgrades: select a building to research upgrades — the Forge improves your soldiers, the Lumber Mill your workers and walls. Upgrade the Great Hall to a Stronghold and a Fortress to unlock higher levels. Upgrade towers to Guard Towers.

Upkeep: a large army costs. From 41 supply your mines give 30% less gold, from 71 supply 60% less. Tap the supply counter to see your upkeep.

Map: tap the map to look there; ⤢ opens it full screen. A red flash at the screen's edge and a red blinking spot on the map show where you are attacked.

Camera: drag to look around, pinch to zoom.
